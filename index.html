Afterlife

Nature takes back what we left behind.

Play in your browser

Free · No sign-up · Works on phones

How it plays

  1. Plant seeds among the ruins: moss, vines, flowers and bamboo.
  2. Drop scrap nearby. Every plant inside its ring grows a step.
  3. Cover the scene, scrap included, until the place is restored. No timer, no losing, always an undo.

Five quiet places

Bus Stop · Rooftop · Petrol Station · Railway Platform · Playground

How it's made

Built with Phaser 4 and TypeScript. Every plant is drawn in code, so no two gardens look alike, and the soundtrack is generated live with Tone.js and blooms as the garden grows. Props are rendered from Kenney's CC0 3D models.
